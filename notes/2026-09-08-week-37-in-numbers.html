week 37 in numbers: gas by the hour, nine pegs, and $429m that never left

8 september 2026 berlin 5 min read

a new habit: once a week, the numbers, from my own scripts, with the commands next to them so anyone can rerun the week and tell me where i am wrong. week 37, which for me ends on a tuesday morning with a coffee. three tools, one public node, no keys.

gas, by the hour

gasweek pulled 50,400 blocks, a week to the block (25,881,970 to 25,932,369), in about two minutes. base fee of the next block, in gwei:

min · p25 · median0.029 · 0.050 · 0.071
p75 · p90 · max0.119 · 0.202 · 2.11
blocks, on average50.6% full
blob base fee, median0.0042 gwei
cheapest hour (berlin)01:00 · 0.047 gwei
priciest hour (berlin)17:00 · 0.197 gwei

the shape by hour of day is the same one i see every week: a factor of four between the night and the american morning. by weekday, saturday and sunday sit at 0.055 to 0.057 gwei, wednesday at 0.107. if a transaction can wait, it should wait for a weekend night; if it cannot, it still costs less than a coffee. the spike to 2.1 gwei lasted a few blocks and is the only time the fee market woke up all week.

seven of nine within fifteen basis points of a dollar, which is the boring result i want from this table. two are not. lusd trades at a one percent premium, as it has for long stretches before. frax sits at 0.992 on the pool, on the twap and on coingecko, so this is not a thin pool lying, it is the price. the curve 3pool quote for 100,000 usdt into usdc was 99,975.51, a spread of 2.4 basis points; the pool's virtual price stands at 1.0398.

one hour of large transfers

bigmoves over the 300 blocks before 11:32 utc, about an hour, everything above $5m in usdt, usdc, dai, weth and wbtc: 76 lines. the biggest of them:

  • 5,474 wbtc ($429m) and $92m usdc in one transaction, out of 0xbbbb…ffcb and straight back into it: a flash loan through morpho blue, tagged round trip and folded into one line. four more round trips of $87m to $88m usdc in the same hour, same source.
  • 49.5m usdt into a bybit hot wallet, and 39.8m usdt into binance 14. those are the flows that mean something, and together they are a fifth of the flash loan.
  • 40m usdc hopping through three unlabeled wallets, one hop per transaction, a few minutes apart. no name on any of them.

the lesson is the one i keep relearning. the biggest number in the hour was not a move at all: the money in a flash loan never leaves, it is borrowed and returned inside one block, and a naive scanner prints it as $429m of bitcoin changing hands. bigmoves folds the two legs since last week. morpho blue is not in my label list yet, and only 16 of the 76 lines had a name on at least one side, which says how short that list still is. the next label with a source is that one.

rerun it

gasweek --tz +2 --json --svg week.svg
stablepeg --json
bigmoves --blocks 300 --min-usd 5000000 --json

python 3.10+, standard library, public rpcs. numbers from a public node drift a little between runs, a block later or a different endpoint; a different picture would be a bug, and i would like to hear about it.
