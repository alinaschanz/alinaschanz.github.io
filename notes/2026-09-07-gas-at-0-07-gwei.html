0.07 gwei. what an empty mainnet costs

7 september 2026 berlin 4 min read

i check gas before bed the way other people check the weather. tonight the base fee on ethereum mainnet was 0.068 gwei. a plain transfer at that price costs a fraction of a cent. a swap costs two or three cents. this is the chain people still call expensive.

the numbers from tonight, pulled around 23:30 berlin time from coingecko, defillama, alternative.me and a public rpc:

btc$79,172 −0.8% 24h
eth$2,488 −0.6% 24h
sol$103.8 −1.8% 24h
eth base fee0.068 gwei
fear & greed71 greed · 62 a week ago
btc dominance59.1%
tvl by chainethereum $49.6b · bsc $5.7b · base $5.6b · arbitrum $1.4b

three things i take from this.

  1. mainnet blockspace is not scarce anymore. most of the day the base fee sits at the floor and the fee market only wakes up for events: a liquidation cascade, a mint, a scare. cheap gas is not a bug. activity moved to the L2s and into blobs, the gas limit went up, and the L1 turned into the settlement layer everyone said it would become. it just happened quietly, so nobody threw a party.
  2. cheap does not mean empty. tvl on ethereum is still almost nine times the next chain. the transactions left, the money stayed. that gap, value on L1 and activity everywhere else, is the thing i keep staring at, because it decides what the L1 actually earns from here.
  3. greed at 71 with btc drifting under 80k is a strange pair. the index went from 62 to 71 in a week while the price went nowhere. sentiment is running ahead of price. it does not have to resolve downwards. it usually resolves, though.

what i am watching this week: the ethereum foundation put two eips on the must-ship list for hegotá. whether the base fee gets a real spike at all this month. and the fund flow numbers, which coinshares reads as people trading the fed path, not people leaving.

not financial advice. just a girl in berlin with a block explorer open at midnight. the numbers above can be rerun with daily_card.py.
