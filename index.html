what i do

read the chain

block explorers, fee markets, flows between wallets and exchanges. the boring public numbers that are usually right before the timeline is.

write it down

a note when a number does not fit the story. once or twice a month, never longer than a coffee. old notes stay up, including the wrong ones.

keep the scripts public

small python, standard library, public endpoints, no keys. anyone can rerun the number instead of trusting the sentence.

that is onchain-notes: daily_card.py, the numbers i look at before posting anything, and gas_now.py, what a transfer or a swap costs on mainnet right now, in cents. the same idea, grown into small packages: gasweek (when is ethereum cheapest, hour by hour, with a chart that redraws itself every night), bigmoves (large stablecoin, weth and wbtc transfers with exchange labels), stablepeg (are the stablecoins still a dollar, read from uniswap and curve) and ens-lookup (names to addresses and back). python 3.10+, standard library, public endpoints, no keys.
