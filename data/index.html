ethereum base fee, one row a day

a dataset that grows by itself. written every night at 00:17 utc by the gasweek workflow, read here straight from the repository.

for every day: the block range, the min, p25, median, p75, p90 and max base fee in gwei, the cheapest and the priciest hour, how full the blocks were and the median blob fee. nothing on this page is stored anywhere but in daily.csv.
