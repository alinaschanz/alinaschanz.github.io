privacy

what this site does with you, which is close to nothing, and the one switch you have.

no cookies, no analytics, no trackers, no font network, no comment widget, no newsletter pixel. i do not know you were here, and i like it that way.

live numbers

the front page, the data page and the scripts page ask public apis for numbers, from your browser, when you open them. each of those requests carries your ip address to that host, the same way any page load does. the hosts are listed below and nowhere else, and the page cannot talk to any other host: a content security policy in every page names exactly these.

live numbers are on.

the switch is stored as one word in your browser's local storage, on your side only. no cookie, nothing sent anywhere.

hosting

the files are served by github pages through cloudflare. both keep the usual server logs (ip address, page, time) for their own operation; i do not receive them and could not read them if i wanted to. the fonts are files on this site, so nothing is fetched from a font provider.

mail

hello@alinaschanz.life is forwarded to a mailbox i read. i keep a mail as long as the conversation lasts and do not share it. encrypt with my key if you prefer.

links to elsewhere

links to x and github are plain links. there is no share button that loads their script, only a link that opens their page when you click it.
